WELCOME TO THE REHEARSAL

Be your own
dream team.

Record one robot at a time. Every saved take replays from the beginning while you record the next robot.

  1. Biggy opens the way. Record a route to the orange pad in Zaal 2. Press E to brace, then save.
  2. Droid brings the power. Record a route to the blue panel in Zaal 11. Press E to connect, then save.
  3. Voxxy starts the show. Record a route through the open door into Zaal 1. Reach the projector and press E, then save.

Use WASD or the arrow keys to move. Press R to record and save. An interaction stays active until you move. Saved robots wait at their last position for the rest of the 18 seconds.

Finally, run the plan. All three jobs must overlap to bring the screen to life. Re-record any take to improve your timing.
