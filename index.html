A SMALL GUIDE TO A BIG NIGHT

Different robots.
Better together.

All three robots travel together. You control one; the others follow, and a robot on a job stays put. Everything reacts to weight: Voxxy weighs 1, Droid 2, Biggy 5.

  1. Stack up. Stand on another robot to weigh more. Voxxy on Droid presses a plate marked 3.
  2. Biggy · 3 Charges through heavy cases with E. With someone on his back, E throws them across gaps. Only Biggy holds plates marked 5, and he breaks weak gratings.
  3. Droid · 2 Connects blue terminals with E. Anywhere else, E makes him lie down as a bridge. Voxxy can cross; Biggy is too heavy.
  4. Voxxy · 1 Jumps twice, bounces high on seat springs, and is the only one who can handle the projector lens.

Move: A / D or arrows. Jump: Space, W or ↑. Ability / doors: E. Switch with 1 / 2 / 3 or the crew cards.

Four stages from the front doors to the keynote room. Collect golden sparks, bounce on rogue cleaning bots and swoopers, jump the sentry bolts, follow the orange signs. Robots left behind are fetched through a service hatch. Energy is shared by the whole crew and refills at checkpoints. Three lives per stage: lose them all and the stage starts over.
